PI Video Publisher

PI Video Publisher helps users connect their TikTok account and publish videos that they choose and confirm.

How it works

  1. User connects TikTok through TikTok Login Kit.
  2. User selects a local video.
  3. User confirms the title, privacy setting, comments, duet, stitch, and AI-generated content setting.
  4. The app uploads the video to TikTok using TikTok Content Posting API.
